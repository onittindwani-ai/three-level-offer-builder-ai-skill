# 3 Level Designs for 16 Industries

Starting ideas only. Prices are left blank on purpose: work them out from your own costs (see PRICING-MATH.md). Only offer what you can really deliver, and only say "limited" if it's true.

## How to adapt a playbook

1. Pick the row closest to your business.
2. Replace each level with what you actually do.
3. Work out the full cost of each level (PRICING-MATH.md).
4. Price from cost and margin, round, check the gaps.
5. Rename in your customers' words (NAMING.md).
6. Write the real reason Level 3 is limited, in one sentence.
