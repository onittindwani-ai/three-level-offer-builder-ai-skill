# Naming Your 3 Levels

Names are the first thing people read on a price list. "Basic, Standard, Premium" says nothing about what the customer gets, and "Basic" quietly tells people it's the bad one.

Tesla named its 2026 Roadster versions **road ready, race ready, air ready**. Each name says who the buyer becomes. That's the idea.

## The rules

1. **About the customer's result or identity,** not about size.
2. **2 or 3 words each,** easy to say out loud on a call.
3. **A clear order:** the names should feel like steps up, so nobody has to guess which is bigger.
4. **The same pattern for all 3** (all "___ ready", or all results, or all speeds).
5. **True.** If a level is called "Done in 48 hours", it must be done in 48 hours.

## 6 naming styles

### 1. Who you become (the Tesla style)
| Business | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Car (Tesla) | Road ready | Race ready | Air ready |
| Gym | Moving | Strong | Competition ready |
| Wedding makeup | Guest ready | Bride ready | Camera ready all day |
| Public speaking coach | Confident | Persuasive | Stage ready |
| Small business website | Online | Found | Booked out |

### 2. The result
| Business | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accountant | Filed | Organised | Growing |
| Dentist (cosmetic) | Fresh | Bright | Wedding ready |
| Cleaning | Tidy | Spotless | Move in ready |
| Marketing agency | Seen | Chosen | Booked out |
| Tutor | Caught up | Confident | Top of class |

### 3. The speed
| Business | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Web design | Live in 3 weeks | Live in 7 days | Live in 48 hours |
| Printing | Standard | Next day | Same day |
| Legal documents | Within 10 days | Within 3 days | Tomorrow |

### 4. Who does it
| Business | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Marketing | Do it yourself kit | Done with you | Done for you |
| Interior design | Plan only | Plan and shopping list | Plan, buy and install |
| Fitness | App plan | Group coaching | 1 on 1 with the head coach |

### 5. The stage of the journey
| Business | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Business coaching | Start | Grow | Scale |
| Software | Try | Run | Rely on it |
| Course | Learn | Practise | Mastered with feedback |

### 6. The moment
| Business | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bakery | Tea time | Birthday | The whole party goes quiet |
| Photography | A few great shots | The full story | The album you'll keep forever |
| Restaurant set menu | Lunch | Date night | The celebration |

## Names to avoid

| Avoid | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Basic / Lite | Tells people it's the worse one; they feel cheap picking it |
| Standard | Says nothing |
| Premium / Pro / Plus | Says "more", not what you get |
| Silver / Gold / Platinum | Jewellery, not results |
| Starter / Growth / Enterprise | Fine for software, but generic; use only if nothing better fits |
| Inside jokes or brand slang | Customers won't understand them |

## Under each name, add one line

The name is the headline; one line under it explains the result:

```
Race ready
For people who want the full experience, every weekend.
```

## Quick test

Read the 3 names to someone outside your business. Ask: "Which one would you pick, and what do you think you'd get?" If they can't answer in 5 seconds, rename.
