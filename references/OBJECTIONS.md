# Answering Price Objections (Without Discounting)

With 3 levels, you rarely need to discount. When someone pushes back, you move them between levels instead of cutting your price.

| They say | Don't | Say instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "It's too expensive." | Drop the price | "Totally fair. [Level 1] gets you [smaller result] for [price], and it comes off [Level 2] if you upgrade within 30 days. Want to start there?" |
| "Can you give me a discount?" | Discount Level 2 | "I keep prices the same for everyone so it's fair. What I can do is [real alternative: off peak slot, smaller scope, payment in 2 parts]." |
| "X charges less." | Attack X | "They might be a great fit. The difference with us is [specific result, speed or guarantee]. If that matters to you, [Level 2] is the one." |
| "I need to think about it." | Push | "Of course. What's the one thing you're unsure about? I'll answer it now so you can decide properly." |
| "I only need a bit of it." | Build a custom quote | "Then [Level 1] is probably right. It covers [what it covers]." |
| "Why is [Level 3] so much more?" | Apologise | "Because I do it myself, in 48 hours, with a direct line to me. I only take [number] a month. Most people are very happy with [Level 2]." |
| "Can I pay later?" | Say yes to everything | "You can pay [deposit]% now to lock your date, and the rest on [real milestone]." (only if you offer it) |
| "Do you have anything cheaper?" | Invent a new option | "[Level 1] is the smallest way to start. Below that, here's a free guide that might help: [real free resource]." |

## Rules

- **Never discount the hero level** without removing something. If you give the same result for less, you train customers to always ask.
- **Move down a level** instead of cutting the price.
- **Offer a real payment option** (deposit, 2 parts) only if you'll honour it.
- **Write your 5 best answers down** and save them as quick replies.
