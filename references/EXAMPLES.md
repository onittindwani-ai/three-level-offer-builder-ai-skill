# 10 Before and After Offers

All businesses, costs and prices below are made up examples to show the method. In real use, only the owner's own numbers are used.

## 1. Home cleaning

**Before:** "Cleaning service: 1,500 per visit." One price, one service. Most customers book once and don't return. Owner works weekends at a thin margin.

**After:**
| Level | Name | What's included | Full cost | Price | Margin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Tidy | 2 hours, 1 cleaner | 918 | 1,699 | 46% |
| 2 | Spotless (recommended) | 3 hours, 2 cleaners, supplies included | 1,938 | 3,599 | 46% |
| 3 | Move in ready | Full deep clean, team of 3, same day, 2 slots a week | 5,151 | 9,499 | 46% |

**Why it works:** the old 1,500 price was below the real cost of a proper clean. Now every level makes money, Spotless is the clear best value, and the same day team has a real limit (2 slots a week).

## 2. Accountant

**Before:** "Bookkeeping packages: Basic 3,000, Standard 6,000, Premium 12,000 per month." Customers compare features and choose Basic.

**After:** Filed (tax return only, 15,000 per year), Organised (monthly books plus tax plus WhatsApp support, 6,500 per month, recommended), Growing (everything plus a quarterly planning call with the partner, 14,000 per month, 5 clients only because the partner does it personally).
**Why it works:** names say what the client gets; Growing's limit is the partner's real time.

## 3. Web designer

**Before:** "Website: 25,000." Long sales calls about pages and features.

**After:** Online (1 page site, live in 2 weeks, 12,000), Found (5 pages plus Google Business setup, live in 7 days, 35,000, recommended), Booked out (full site, copywriting and booking system, live in 48 hours, 90,000, 1 build at a time).
**Why it works:** speed and result separate the levels; the 48 hour build is genuinely limited.

## 4. Bakery

**Before:** Price list of 40 cakes by weight.

**After:** Tea time (single cakes, 450 to 900), Birthday (custom cake for up to 20, 2,800, recommended), The whole party goes quiet (tiered showpiece with delivery and setup, 12,000, 3 per weekend because of oven size).
**Why it works:** 40 choices became 3 moments; the showpiece's limit is physical.

## 5. Fitness coach

**Before:** "Personal training 1,200 per session."

**After:** Moving (group classes plus app plan, 2,500 a month), Strong (classes plus monthly coach check in, 4,500 a month, recommended), Competition ready (2 sessions a week 1 on 1 with the head coach, 18,000 a month, 6 clients at a time).
**Why it works:** the old per session price made people buy 1 session and stop; monthly levels build habits and revenue.

## 6. Marketing agency

**Before:** Custom quote for every client, 2 week proposal process.

**After:** Seen (audit plus 30 day plan, 25,000 one time, credited to Chosen within 30 days), Chosen (monthly done with you campaigns, 75,000 a month, recommended), Booked out (done for you with weekly calls, 2,00,000 a month, by application, 3 clients at a time).
**Why it works:** the audit is a real first step that leads to Chosen; Booked out is limited by team capacity.

## 7. Photographer

**Before:** "Packages from 20,000." People ask "what's included?" on every call.

**After:** A few great shots (1 hour, 25 edited photos, 15,000), The full story (half day, 150 edited photos, 45,000, recommended), The album you'll keep forever (full day, 2 photographers, printed album, 1,10,000, limited by weekend dates).

## 8. IT support

**Before:** Pay per visit only, 1,500 per call out.

**After:** Fix it (pay per fix, next day, 1,800), Covered (monthly plan for up to 10 laptops, same day response, 12,000 a month, recommended), Never down (1 hour response, quarterly security check, 30,000 a month, 4 clients per engineer).

## 9. Online store (candles)

**Before:** Single candles only, 799 each.

**After:** Single candle (799), The trio (3 scents that work together, 1,999, best value, recommended), The gift box (trio plus premium box, handwritten note, express delivery, 3,499, made in batches of 50).

## 10. Consultant

**Before:** "1,500 per hour, minimum 3 hours."

**After:** Clarity (1 strategy session with a written plan, 9,000), Plan and progress (3 months, fortnightly calls, 75,000, recommended), Done with me (6 months, weekly calls, direct WhatsApp, 3,00,000, by application, 4 clients at a time).
**Why it works:** clients stop counting hours and start buying results.
