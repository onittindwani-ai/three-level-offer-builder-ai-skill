# Template: Answering "How Much?" (WhatsApp, DM, Email, Phone)

When someone asks the price, give it. Hiding it ("depends, share your requirement") loses people who are messaging 3 businesses at once. With 3 levels you answer clearly and guide them to the right one.

## Fill in version

```
Hi [name]! We have 3 options:

[Level 2 name]: [price]. [One line result.] Most people pick this one.
[Level 3 name]: [price]. [The difference: faster / personal / certain.] I only take [number] a month.
[Level 1 name]: [price]. [Smaller result.] A good way to start.

Which sounds closest to what you need? Or tell me [the 1 detail you need] and I'll recommend one.
```

Order on purpose: the hero first, the premium second (it makes the hero look reasonable), the easy yes last (a safe way in).

## Example (cleaning)

```
Hi Priya! We have 3 options:

Spotless: 3,599. 3 hours, 2 cleaners, supplies included. Most people pick this one.
Move in ready: 9,499. A full deep clean, same day, team of 3. Only 2 slots a week.
Tidy: 1,699. 2 hours, 1 cleaner. A good way to start.

Which sounds closest? Or send me your flat size and I'll recommend one.
```

## Short version (when they ask for one price only)

```
[Level 2 name] is [price] and covers [result]. There's also a smaller option at [Level 1 price] and a full premium one at [Level 3 price]. Want the details?
```

## Rules

- Answer within the first line.
- Say which one most people choose (only if true) or which you recommend.
- One question at the end, easy to answer.
- Save it as a quick reply so every customer gets the same, clear answer.
