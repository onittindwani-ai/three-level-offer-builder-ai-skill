# Template: Pricing Page or Price List

The pricing page is where people decide. It should make Level 2 the obvious choice in about 10 seconds.

## Structure

```
1. HEADLINE          The result, not "Pricing" ("Pick how fast you want your new website")
2. 3 COLUMNS         Level 1 | Level 2 (marked Recommended, slightly larger) | Level 3
3. IN EACH COLUMN    Name, one line result, price, 4 to 6 included items, timeline, one button
4. NOT INCLUDED      One plain line under each column
5. COMPARE           (optional) A short table of the 3 to 5 differences that matter
6. QUESTIONS         Deposit, refund, payment options, "which one is right for me?"
7. NOT SURE?         One small step: "Book a free 10 minute call and I'll tell you which fits."
```

## Fill in version (one column)

```
[LEVEL NAME]
[One line result: "For people who want ___ in ___"]
[PRICE] [per month / one time] [tax included or + tax]
✓ [included item with a number or detail]
✓ [included item]
✓ [included item]
✓ [timeline: "Live in 7 days"]
Not included: [one line]
[BUTTON: "Start with [Level name]"]
```

For Level 3, add the honest limit under the price:
```
By application. I take [number] a month because [real reason].
```

## Rules

- **3 columns, not 4 or 5.** Anything custom goes in one line: "Need something bigger? Let's talk."
- **Level 2 in the middle,** visually highlighted. Use "Recommended", or "Most popular" only if it's true.
- **Real prices.** If it varies, say "from" plus what it depends on. Avoid "contact us for pricing" unless it truly can't be estimated.
- **Same order of features** in each column, so people can compare across.
- **Deposit and refund** in one sentence each, near the buttons.
- **Mobile first:** on a phone, show Level 2 first, then Level 3, then Level 1 (or let people swipe).

## Common mistakes

- Feature lists of 20 items with ticks and crosses.
- "Basic" as a name.
- A different button text in each column.
- Hidden fees found at checkout.
- No way to ask "which one is right for me?"
