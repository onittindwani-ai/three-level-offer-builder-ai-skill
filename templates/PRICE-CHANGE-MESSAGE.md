# Template: Telling Customers About New Prices

New levels often mean a price rise. Done well, most customers stay. Done badly (surprise, no notice, no reason), you lose trust.

## Message to existing customers (fill in)

```
Hi [name],

From [date, at least 30 days away], our prices are changing.

What's new: we now have 3 options, so you can choose what fits:
- [Level 1 name]: [price]. [result]
- [Level 2 name]: [price]. [result]
- [Level 3 name]: [price]. [result]

For you: [the fair thing: "you keep your current price until [date]" / "you move to [Level 2] at your current price for the next 3 months"].

Why: [one honest sentence: "our costs for [x] have gone up" / "we've added [real improvement]"].

Nothing to do if you're happy. If you'd like to switch options, just reply.

Thank you for being with us,
[name]
```

## Rules

- **Notice:** at least 30 days, longer for contracts. Check what your contracts say.
- **One honest reason.** No long apology.
- **Something fair for loyal customers:** a grace period, a locked price for a while, or an upgrade.
- **Make switching levels easy:** one reply.
- **Don't change the price in the middle of a paid period.**
