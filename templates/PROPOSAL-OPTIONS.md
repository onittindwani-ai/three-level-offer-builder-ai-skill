# Template: Options Inside a Proposal or Quote

Giving one price in a proposal turns it into a yes or no decision. Giving 3 options turns it into "which one?", and most people pick the middle.

## Fill in version

```
3 ways we can do this

OPTION A: [Level 1 name], [price]
[What they get, 2 to 3 lines]
Best if: [situation]

OPTION B: [Level 2 name], [price]  (Recommended)
[What they get, 3 to 4 lines]
Best if: [situation, the most common one]
Why we recommend it: [one sentence tied to what they told you]

OPTION C: [Level 3 name], [price]
[What they get, 3 to 4 lines]
Best if: [situation]
Note: [real limit and reason]

Next step: Reply with A, B or C by [real date] and we'll start on [date].
```

## Rules

- **Tie the recommendation to their words:** "You said speed matters before your launch, so B gets you live in 7 days."
- **Same structure for each option,** so they compare fairly.
- **Option A must still deliver something real,** not a crippled version designed to fail.
- **One dated next step.**
- **Payment terms once,** at the end: deposit, schedule, refund rule.
