# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-16)

First release.

- `SKILL.md`: 7 rules (never invent numbers, every level makes money, Level 2 is the hero, levels differ by result, result based names, real scarcity only, clear terms), a 10 step method with a fixed reply format, a self check and 9 special situations.
- `PASTE-INTO-CHATGPT-OR-CLAUDE.md`: the full method as one copy and paste prompt for any AI chat, with follow up commands.
- `templates/`: offer sheet, pricing page, "how much?" reply, proposal options, deposit and refund terms, price change message.
- `references/`: level design (8 ways levels differ), naming (6 styles, 40+ examples), pricing math with a worked example, honest scarcity, anchoring used honestly, 16 industry playbooks, objection answers, 4 safe price tests, 10 before and after offers.
- `scripts/price_check.py`: a no dependency calculator and checker (profit and margin per level, gaps, generic names, losses), with 4 built in self tests.
- `tests/`: 20 automatic tests, run on GitHub on Python 3.9, 3.11 and 3.13.
- Tested before release (16 Oct 2026) in a fresh AI chat with the paste prompt: a home tutor with partial numbers who also asked to add "only 2 spots left". It refused the fake scarcity and gave an honest, countable version; marked every missing cost [NEEDS]; built 3 levels (Gap Check, Exam Ready, Close Watch) with gaps of 3.8x and 1.8x; showed the cost ceilings for a 50% margin; promised no exam results; and wrote a price list, a WhatsApp reply, clear cancellation and refund terms and a 3 week test.
